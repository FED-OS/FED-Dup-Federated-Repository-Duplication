# Pricing

Fed-Dup is **free and open-source software** licensed under the MIT License.
There are no paid tiers, no feature gates, and no usage limits — the entire
project is available to everyone at no cost.

This document describes the project's **funding model** and how you can
optionally support its ongoing development.

---

## Core Principle

> **Fed-Dup is and will remain free.**

All features — the engine, web UI, background worker, Docker deployment, CI/CD
pipelines, and documentation — are available to all users at no cost. There
are no "premium" features, no freemium tiers, and no plans to introduce them.

---

## Why Sponsor?

Fed-Dup is built and maintained by volunteers. Sponsorship helps:

- **Cover infrastructure costs** — domains, CI/CD runner minutes, container
  registry hosting.
- **Reward maintainers** for their time reviewing issues, triaging bugs, and
  merging PRs.
- **Fund feature development** — sponsored features get prioritized on the
  [Roadmap](ROADMAP.md).
- **Ensure long-term sustainability** — open-source projects without funding
  often go unmaintained. Your support helps prevent that.

---

## Sponsorship Tiers

Sponsorship is handled via [Ko-fi](https://ko-fi.com/feddup). These are
*guidelines* — you can contribute any amount.

| Tier              | Suggested Amount | What It Supports                                   |
|-------------------|------------------|----------------------------------------------------|
| ☕ Coffee          | $5 / month       | A thank-you; covers a day of CI runner minutes.    |
| 🍕 Pizza          | $25 / month      | Bug triage and small fixes; listed in AUTHORS.     |
| 🛡️ Shield         | $100 / month     | Feature prioritization; listed as a Supporter.     |
| 🏢 Organization   | $500+ / month    | Enterprise guidance, priority issue triage,        |
|                   |                  | roadmap influence; listed as an Org Supporter.     |

### What sponsors get

- **Recognition** — listed in [AUTHORS.md](AUTHORS.md) and the README
  (optional — you can remain anonymous).
- **Priority triage** — sponsored issues and feature requests are reviewed
  first.
- **Roadmap influence** — sponsors can vote on which roadmap items to
  prioritize.
- **Good karma** — you are helping keep a useful tool free for everyone. 😊

### What sponsors do *not* get

- **No exclusive features** — everything is open-source and available to all.
- **No SLAs or guarantees** — sponsorship is voluntary support, not a
  service contract.
- **No preferential licensing** — the MIT License applies equally to all.

## For Organizations

Organizations that rely on Fed-Dup in production are encouraged to sponsor at
the 🏢 Organization tier. Benefits include:

- Priority issue triage (target: 48-hour first response).
- Influence on the project roadmap.
- Public recognition as an Org Supporter.
- The knowledge that you are sustaining a tool your team depends on.

For custom sponsorship arrangements (e.g., funding a specific feature),
contact the maintainers via [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/feddup/fed-dup/discussions)
or the Ko-fi message system.

---

## Financial Transparency

Fed-Dup is a lightweight project with modest costs:

| Expense                | Estimated Annual Cost |
|------------------------|-----------------------|
| CI/CD runner minutes   | $0–100 (GitHub free tier covers most) |
| Container registry     | $0 (GitHub Packages free tier)       |
| Domain                 | $10–20                                |
| Total                  | ~$20–120/year                         |

Sponsorship beyond these costs goes directly to compensating maintainers for
their time. We aim to be transparent about how funds are used.

## Alternative Ways to Help

Can't sponsor financially? You can still help enormously:

- ⭐ **Star the repository** — increases visibility.
- 🐛 **Report bugs** — detailed bug reports are invaluable.
- 💡 **Suggest features** — tell us what you need.
- 📝 **Improve docs** — documentation PRs are always welcome.
- 🧪 **Write tests** — help increase coverage.
- 📣 **Spread the word** — mention Fed-Dup in blog posts, talks, or social
  media.
- 🤝 **Answer questions** in GitHub Discussions.

See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) for how to contribute code and docs.

---

## Commercial Services

Fed-Dup does not currently offer paid commercial services (hosting, support
contracts, custom development). If there is sufficient demand, this may change
in the future — but the core tool will always remain free and open-source.

If you need enterprise-grade Git backup with SLAs, consider commercial
providers in the space. Fed-Dup is designed for self-hosted, community-driven
use cases.
